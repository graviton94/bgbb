## English

Effective: 2026-10-24 · App: Pocket Toot (io.github.graviton94.bgbb)

Pocket Toot does not collect personal data. There is no server and the app itself sends nothing. The only exception is the optional rewarded ad (Google AdMob), which sends the information listed under “Ads” to Google.

- **On your device only:** your records (loud, buzz and held counts), earned titles, and your friend, sound and hours settings stay on this phone. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.
- **What isn't there:** no account, no server, no analytics or tracking of our own. Apart from ads the app works offline; its fonts, pictures and sounds are all inside the app.
- **Ads:** a Google AdMob rewarded ad is shown only when you tap “Watch an ad +3” after using up your meme draws (no ads anywhere else). To serve it, the AdMob SDK sends Google the advertising ID, approximate location (from IP), device and app information, ad interactions and crash information, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Where consent is required (e.g. the EU) you are asked first. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your phone settings. More: Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and How Google uses information from partner sites (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Reading the phone mode:** when a surprise prank goes off, the app reads only the ringer mode (sound, vibrate, silent) and whether Do Not Disturb is on, to decide the result. It is used for that record only and is not stored or sent.
- **Microphone:** the microphone permission is requested only when you tap “Record my voice”, and records for 3 seconds. The recording stays on this phone, is played only as a prank sound, and is sent nowhere.
- **Alarms:** the app uses the phone's alarm service to make sounds within your hours, and sets them again after a restart.
- **Notifications:** the app asks for notification permission to show one silent line right after each one. The text is made on the phone and sent nowhere. Turn it off with “Notification after each one” in Settings.
- **Payments:** support and coin payments are handled by Google Play. The developer never receives payment details. The app only asks Google Play which items you have supported with.
- **Saving the news card:** only when you tap “Share the news”, one image is saved to your photos (Pictures/방구뿡) and handed to the app you choose. Nothing goes to the developer.
- **Feedback &amp; error reports:** tapping “Send feedback” in Settings opens your own mail app, pre-filled with app version, device model, Android version and language (plus the error text if the app hit an error). It reaches the developer only if you send it yourself, and never includes your records or recordings. It is used only to reply and fix bugs, and is not shared.
- **Backup:** if device backup is on, records and settings may be included in your encrypted Google backup. This is used only when moving to a new phone.
- **Deleting:** “Erase everything” in Settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything from the device.
- **Children:** not directed at children under 13.

### Deleting data
There is no account and the app sends no records to any server, so the developer holds nothing to delete. Records on the device are removed with Settings → “Erase everything” or by uninstalling; the advertising ID can be reset or deleted in phone settings. Contact: ruahn49@gmail.com
